Why PyroView?

Satellite images are powerful. Understanding them matters.

Looking at two images side by side can be difficult without context. PyroView gives visitors a guided way to compare the available imagery, explore terrain, and understand what the visualisation does—and does not—show.

From a map you can move, to evidence you can understand.

PyroView combines a before-and-after view with terrain controls and explanations, so exploration has a clear purpose instead of being just a collection of map tools.

Responsible interpretation

What PyroView can—and cannot—tell you.

Good geospatial tools make their evidence and limitations visible.

What you can explore

  • The displayed pre-fire RGB image and post-fire NBR visualisation.
  • The difference in appearance between the two dated image products.
  • The surrounding terrain context and map framing.

What you should not assume

  • This page does not provide an official burn-severity classification.
  • The displayed colours alone do not verify severity at a location.
  • The two image dates do not, by themselves, establish the cause of every visible change.
